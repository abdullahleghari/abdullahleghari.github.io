Family values · Heritage · Service

Hafiz Abdullah Khalid Khan Leghari Baloch was born on 19 March 1958 in Choti Zehrein. His family roots are connected to the Leghari Baloch heritage, and his permanent address is in Raheema Baad, Tehsil Sadiqabad, District Rahim Yar Khan.

He served as a Senior Joint Director with the Pakistan Airports Authority and is now retired. This profile celebrates his life, his professional service and the cultural heritage that forms an important part of his identity.

The land of Balochistan

A heritage rich in history and natural beauty

Balochistan is known for its ancient archaeological sites, striking landscapes, traditional craftsmanship, poetry and music. Here are a few highlights of its remarkable heritage.

01 / ANCIENT HISTORY

Mehrgarh

One of South Asia's earliest known farming settlements, with archaeological remains dating to around 7000 BCE.

02 / LANDSCAPE

Hingol National Park

A remarkable natural landscape featuring rugged mountains, unusual rock formations, mud volcanoes and coastal scenery.

03 / CRAFTSMANSHIP

Balochi Embroidery

Recognised for intricate geometric patterns, detailed handwork and colourful designs passed down through generations.

04 / MUSIC & POETRY

Living Traditions

Balochistan's cultural expression includes folk poetry, storytelling and traditional instruments such as the saroz and dambura.

05 / NATURAL HERITAGE

Ziarat Juniper Forest

The Ziarat region is celebrated for its ancient juniper woodlands and peaceful mountain scenery.

06 / COASTAL BEAUTY

Makran Coast

A dramatic stretch along the Arabian Sea, known for its beaches, cliffs, fishing communities and scenic coastal routes.
